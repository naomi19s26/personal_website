Passion Projects

What I’m building

A few of the ideas and experiences I’m exploring at the intersection of product, AI, and learning.

CogniCount

As AI becomes a bigger part of how we think, learn, and work, I’m interested in understanding how much of our own thinking we’re still doing. I’m building CogniCount to help people understand how AI is affecting the skills they care about while staying more aware of their own cognitive engagement.

Private Equity Lifecycle Simulation

Private equity is one of the more underdeveloped areas of experiential business education, and I wanted to help bring that experience into the classroom. I’m building an interactive simulation that gives students a chance to experience the decisions, competition, and tradeoffs involved in private equity investing.

Unified Workday

Applying to jobs often means repeatedly entering the same information into different Workday applications, even when the information hasn’t changed. After talking with people who use Workday, I wanted to explore a more centralized experience that could reuse a user’s information while still adapting to each company’s specific requirements.
